About the Book

Two civilizations that never met each wrote a manual for the same journey.

The Egyptian Book of the Dead and the Tibetan Book of the Dead (the Bardo Thodol) both describe what waits for the soul after death: the passages, the judgment, the lights, and the beings met along the way.

The Third Book of the Dead reads these two ancient texts side by side. Chapter by chapter it reveals their striking overlaps, then brings in a witness nobody expected: modern ufology.

Through Hermeticism, Gnosticism, and Kabbalah, the book builds toward one coherent picture of what this reality actually is, what happens after death, and what life is for.

It includes a genuine revelation about the true name of Jesus, pieced together from ancient sources in a way that has not been presented before. Beyond theory, it offers practical ways to apply these systems and glimpses into the mechanism behind manifestation.

This is not a book for everyone. If it has found its way to you, read on.

About the Author

I come from a sacred order. This book, however, is not affiliated with that order, which is why I will not name it specifically.

The people who helped

Three others from the order helped me bring this book into existence. The Labyrinthian on X took on the complete marketing and promotion of the book. Another, who prefers not to be named, creates the music and graphic work. Sefra Banja served as the editor.

None of us had prior experience making a book. The Labyrinthian in particular shared a great deal of knowledge that has been incorporated into the work itself.

None of the people who helped wanted to be paid. They did this freely, out of the goodness of their hearts.

Personal circumstances

I myself live with rare conditions that severely limit what I can do physically and in daily life. Without the help of my fraters and sorors, this book would not exist. Despite the knowledge I carry, not every condition, especially genetic ones, can be healed through that knowledge alone.

How the book grew

Originally the book was much smaller and far more personal in nature. During the writing process, however, new revelations kept emerging that belonged in it. As a result the book grew and kept growing, eventually exceeding 300 pages.

The final result is a substantial work written in such a way that, in principle, anyone can understand it. We took the time to explain the various mystical systems clearly, without going deeper than necessary. Only what is needed to follow the rest of the book and its arguments is included.

Because of this, the book has become filled with revelations that are usually not shared with the general public. It also contains one earth-shattering revelation about Jesus himself that, to my knowledge, has never been described in this way before.

What the book covers

A number of subjects are woven together throughout the text. We begin with the nature of the reality we currently inhabit, touching on simulation theory and Gnosticism. We discuss the Archons, or the Watchers from the Book of Enoch, and then look at them through a modern lens: could the beings described in those ancient accounts be what we now call aliens?

From there we move to life and the soul itself. Why we live, the true meaning of life, and what all of this actually is. The final part, and the reason for the book’s title, gives instructions on what to do at the moment of death, drawn from different cultures.

Because so much background information had to be provided, explaining systems such as Hermeticism, Kabbalah, and others, the book has also become a solid introduction for those who are new to the world of mysticism, the occult, and spirituality. For readers who wish to walk the path of the initiate, I refer to other books and offer guidance on how to go further.

Alongside the theoretical knowledge, much of the material is practically applicable and can be used to improve one’s life. Everything is approached in a relatively scientific manner. People of any background, culture, or religion should be able to find something of value in these pages. We give extensive argumentation for why things are the way they are. Sometimes the arguments are stronger, sometimes weaker. Everyone is free to take from the book what resonates with them.

In short: I do not impose a belief system on you.
